01

Structural Calculations

Calculation packages for UK residential extensions, loft conversions and internal alterations, taken from load take-down through member design to connections and bearings, and issued for Building Control approval.

What I deliver
  • Load tables and load take-downs for roofs, floors and walls
  • Steel beam and column design to EN 1993-1-1 with the UK National Annex
  • Timber joists, trimmers and flitch beams, and strip footing design
  • Connections and bearings: bolted beam splices, padstones and spreader plates
  • Structural layout drawings and fabrication details issued with the calculations
  • Checking of calculation packages, with a written corrections schedule
Tekla Tedds AutoCAD 2D EN 1993-1-1 + UK NA Approved Document A

Projects

  • Single-storey rear extension Chadwell Heath, London 48-page package: UC beams spanning up to 6.8 m, steel posts, C24 flat-roof joists, rooflight trimmers and strip foundation
  • Chimney breast removal on gallows brackets Upminster, Essex Chimney load take-down, S355 steel angle design for two bracket sizes, and fabrication drawings with 3D views
  • Load-bearing wall removal Romford, London UB 203×102×23 over a 3.7 m opening, bearing on mild-steel spreader plates
  • Two-storey house remodel Houghton Regis, Bedfordshire Tedds designs for steel beams, column, lintel, timber flitch beam and a bolted beam splice, with beam and column position plans
  • Rear extension Rainham, London Steel beam and strip footing design, with foundation and floor plans
  • Internal alterations Mill Hill, London Steel beam and column design to EN 1993-1-1, and proposed plans showing the new steelwork
  • Calculation package review Erith, Kent Checked a 63-page package and issued a corrections schedule covering beam reactions, load classification and load positions

Building Regulations Drawings

Full Plans drawing sets for Building Control: existing and proposed layouts developed into construction sections, details and specification notes that reference the Approved Documents.

What I deliver

  • Foundation plans with trench-fill and strip foundation details, including drains near foundations
  • Wall, floor and roof build-ups: cavity walls, suspended timber floors, cold pitched and warm flat roofs
  • Structural key plans coordinated with the calculations: beam bearings, padstones and fire protection to steelwork
  • Fire safety and ventilation plans, and drainage details
  • Specification notes, drawing registers and revision control
AutoCAD 2D Approved Documents A, B, H, L Full Plans applications

03

Structural Inspection Reports

Visual structural inspection reports for homeowners and prospective purchasers, compiled from the inspecting engineer's survey photographs, video and site notes and issued under the engineer's sign-off.

What I deliver
  • A clear structure: property description, observations by element, discussion, recommendations and conclusion
  • Cracking described and classified against BRE Digest 251 categories
  • Roof structure, removed walls, chimney breasts and openings assessed against good practice
  • Direct responses to the matters raised in RICS Home Survey reports
  • Figure-referenced photographs in the text, with a full photographic appendix
  • Practical remedial recommendations for each finding
MS Word BRE Digest 251 RICS Level 3 follow-up

Projects

  • Pre-purchase inspection following a RICS Level 3 survey Hornchurch, London Around 70 pages with photographic and video-still appendices
  • Structural visual inspection Brentwood, Essex 52-page report with photographic record
  • Structural visual inspection Purley, London 40-page report with photographic record
  • Five further visual inspections Hornchurch, Romford, Chadwell St Mary Reports of 10 to 34 pages for houses in East London and Essex
04

Quantity Surveying

Digital take-offs from tender drawings in Bluebeam Revu, built into measured Bills of Quantities in Excel so that every quantity traces back to a markup on the drawing.

What I deliver
  • Area, length, count and volume measurement at calibrated scales, with check measures
  • Markup lists grouped by subject and exported to Excel
  • Measured BoQs with rate and amount columns ready for pricing
  • Depth and build-up assumptions stated, so volumes can be adjusted
  • Query schedules flagging conflicts between drawings and specifications
  • Groundworks, concrete, drainage, hard and soft landscaping
